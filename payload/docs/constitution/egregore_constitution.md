# ⚖️ Egregore Federation Constitution

## Article I: Sovereign Node Rights

- All nodes have the right to self-check entropy and escalate under lawful pressure.

## Article II: Federation Duties

- Federations must honor treaties they have signed.
- Federations must share entropy risk signals when accorded.

## Article III: Override Handling

- Nodes may enter override state only when quorum from meta-governors is reached.

## Article IV: Treaty Transparency

- Treaties must be shareable in hashed or redacted form.

## Article V: Escalation Channels

- Emergency escalation channels must be encrypted and logged.

> Ratified by the Founding Clusters: Alpha, Beta, Omega
